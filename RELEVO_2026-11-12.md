**A0. Derivation and tests (done).** The average of |D - rho cos(theta)|, the kernel G, its
derivative and the equations of motion (ReducedL) were derived again and agree with the notes.
Derived a third time, independently, by the successor (Opus 5.5): same kernel, same
dG/drho = sqrt(1 - d^2/rho^2), same equations; and dG/dd = asin(d/rho), so
1/2 + asin(d/rho)/pi, the weight of the enclosed-mass term of test P7, is the fraction of the
radial period that the other shell spends inside.
`reproducir/scripts/reducido_pruebas.py` holds the tests, which all pass (one minute):

| test | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G against the direct average over theta, 200 pairs (rho, d) | 6e-16 |
| P2 | dG/drho against sqrt(1 - d^2/rho^2) | 1e-8 |
| P3 | `campofl` (C, single precision) against the direct sum in double precision | 2e-7 |
| P4 | equal L: `campofl` against `campof`, `energial` against `energia` | 1e-7, 2e-15 |
| P5 | the sum of the equation of motion is the gradient of the interaction energy | 3e-8 |
| P6 | 720 particles, N_L = 4, to t = 1000: dH/U with dt = 5 and 2.5; dP/P | 1.2e-4, 2.9e-5; 4e-7 |
| P7 | two shells, exact equations against the reduced model of the same pair | below |

P7 is new and does not involve the PIC code or a radial grid. Two shells (masses 0.002,
J = 0.004 and 0.001) are integrated with the exact equations (EOM), each crossing resolved as
an event, during 100 radial periods. With equal L the model gives the exchanged action within
2 %. With different L (d = 0.25 and 0.5 times the initial |zeta_1 - zeta_2|) the relative phase
of the shells turns 1.8 and 6.0 % faster in the model. The error drops to 0.4 and 1.5 % when
each shell gets the frequency shift by the mass it encloses on average,
0.1454 m_other [1/2 + asin(d/rho)/pi]. With equal L this shift is the same for all shells (it
is the uniform shift of the notes) and drops out; with distributed L it does not. **This is
the leading term that Eq. (ReducedL) omits.** Evaluated on the stored runs of the model it
reduces the differential rotation between shells of different L by 5 to 11 % at
M_gas/M = 0.03 for t >= 10 tau_1 (20 to 25 % in the initial compact cloud) and by 2 % at 0.01.
It opposes dOmega/dL, so the PIC code should show cores that are somewhat more robust than
those of the model. It is not implemented in `reducido.py` (only in the pair test, option
`corr` of `reducido_par`). A Hamiltonian way to include it would be the kernel
G(rho, d_ij + beta (J_i - J_j)); not tried, and for equal L the frequency measurements of the
notes bound any J-dependent part of the shift to 5 % of the shift, so do not expect it to
close the gap between model and PIC at equal L.

## 7. Known pitfalls

- `grep` is a wrapper that returns nothing in some directories: use `command grep`. A
  foreground `sleep` is blocked. A command that exceeds 600 s is moved to the background:
  launch long jobs with a chain script (`exe/demo_eta/bulto/cadena_tanda5.sh` is the pattern),
  with `date` between runs and `FIN` at the end.
- Erik's plan reaches its usage limit during long turns. Write notes and commit early.
- Wall time on the laptop varies by a factor 2 with 4 threads.
- a_c from the particles selected at the last snapshot is not reliable in long runs; use
  `reducido.centro_instantaneo`.
- The pair sum in double precision is ten times slower than in single precision (error 1e-6).
  With distributed L only the single-precision sum exists.
- The tables of the notes are macros (`\TabL`, `\TabReferencia`, ...) loaded in the preamble;
  an `\input` inside a `tabular` fails. `main.pdf` of each document is added with `git add -f`.
- The series `hk?.tl` written by the codes carry the factor a_n of the test function and use
  the map of the external potential. The analysis of the cloud does not use them.
- A lattice in L recurs, and the recurrence is felt long before t_rec (Section 5.A). Check
  every lattice with `nube_L.py reticulas`.
- `reducido.diagnosticos` reproduces its stored files bit by bit only with
  `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`; with more threads the BLAS product changes the last bit of Zc, ac, dr.
- The stored runs of the model with equal L have no key `L`.
- In `_sp`, `__pycache__` folders used to be tracked by mistake; they were removed from the
  repository on 2026-11-12 and are ignored now.
- `nube_L.py` keeps the interval of L of the runs with a single L at [1.5, 2.5]: it only sets
  the cell size dlc, which cancels, but changing it changes the last bits of the masses.
